# English language bank

Sentences below are taken from HKU Faculty of Education sample consent forms and approved HREC applications. Copy them verbatim and replace `{{variables}}`. Sentences in `[square brackets]` are conditional: keep only when the condition holds, then remove the brackets.

Section headings in the sectioned format are ALL CAPS. Keep the order.

---

## §A　Adult participant, sectioned format（PI is staff; teachers, adults, university students）

```
Informed Consent Form for Adult

{{title_en}}

You are invited to participate in a research study conducted by {{pi_title}} {{pi_name}} in the {{pi_dept}}.

PURPOSE OF THE STUDY

This study aims to {{purpose_lay}}. [It also examines {{secondary_aim}}.]

PROCEDURES

If you agree to participate, you will {{procedures}}. [The interview will be conducted in {{language}}.] [With your prior consent, the interview will be audio-recorded for transcription and analysis purposes only.] [In addition, {{other_data}} will be collected and analysed as part of the study.] The data collection will take place between {{period}}. In total, your participation will take approximately {{total_time}}.

POTENTIAL RISKS / DISCOMFORTS AND THEIR MINIMIZATION

The study involves no more than minimal risk; the probability and magnitude of any discomfort are no greater than those ordinarily encountered in daily life. [Some questions ask about your personal views and experiences. You are free to skip any question you do not wish to answer and to end the interview at any time.] [Because the researcher is also {{relationship}}, your decision to participate or not will have no effect on {{grades_assessment_relationship}}.]

COMPENSATION FOR PARTICIPATION

Not applicable.
　or
Each participant will receive {{amount}} to offset the time and travel involved.

POTENTIAL BENEFITS

{{benefits_individual}} For the wider community, the findings are expected to {{benefits_wider}}. [A copy of the research results will be given to you upon request after the project is completed.]

CONFIDENTIALITY

All the information that is obtained during this research project will be kept strictly confidential, and your privacy will be protected to the maximum extent allowable by law. The information obtained in the study will be used for research purposes only and will be accessed by the research team only. All collected data (including {{data_types}}) will be stored on {{storage}}. In research reports, theses, or any public publications, pseudonyms will replace participants' real names, and any personal information that could potentially identify you (e.g., your name, specific school names, workplace locations, or other identifying details) will be removed or generalized. [Your de-identified information could be used for future research without additional informed consent.] [To match the data from different sources, you will be assigned a unique code; the key linking the code to your identity will be stored separately and destroyed once matching is complete.]

DATA RETENTION

Data containing personal identifiers will be securely retained for {{retention_identifiable}} after the publication of the first paper resulting from this research. After this period, all files containing personal identifiers will be permanently and securely destroyed. [Anonymised data will be retained for {{retention_anonymised}}.]

PARTICIPATION AND WITHDRAWAL

Your participation is voluntary. This means that you can choose to stop at any time without negative consequences. [You have the right to review the audio recording(s) and request the deletion of part or all of the recording(s).] [You have the right to review your data and request the deletion of part or all of it.] If you withdraw, any data already collected from you will be deleted upon your request.

QUESTIONS AND CONCERNS

If you have any questions about the research, please feel free to contact {{pi_title}} {{pi_name}} (telephone: {{pi_phone}} / email: {{pi_email}}). If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the Human Research Ethics Committee, HKU (3917-5267).

SIGNATURE

I _________________________________ (Name of Participant) understand the procedures described above and agree to participate in this study.

[I ** agree / do not agree to the audio-recording during the procedure.]

[I ** wish / do not wish to be identified.]　　(only when the procedure involves a personal interview)

(** Please delete as appropriate.)

Signature of Participant: ______________________　　Date: ______________

Date of Preparation: {{date_of_preparation}}
HREC Approval Expiration date:
HREC Reference Number: [The reference number is indicated in the letter of approval for ethical clearance issued by the Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC).]
```

Variants:

- **Online interview, e-consent**: replace the signature block with `Please reply to this email with "I agree" / type your full name below to indicate consent.` and keep the two agree/do-not-agree lines as questions.
- **Two-tier study (core + optional interview)**: split into Consent Form A and B, each with its own PROCEDURES and SIGNATURE. Title them `Consent Form A: {{scope}}`.

## §B　Teacher / professor, letter format（researcher is a student）

```
[Date]

[School name & address]

Dear teacher,

I am enrolled in the {{student_programme}} programme in the Faculty of Education, the University of Hong Kong. I am conducting a research project titled "{{title_en}}". The aim of the project is to {{purpose_lay}}. I am writing to invite you to participate.

If you agree to participate in this project, [for intervention] you will try out {{materials}} designed to {{intervention_purpose}}. [for lesson observations] You will be observed for {{n}} of your {{subject}} lessons (in {{grade}}). These lessons will be {{audio/video}}-recorded, and I will also jot down field notes during lesson observations. [for interviews] You will be invited to {{an individual / a group}} interview about {{interview_topics}}, which will take place {{location}} and will last for around {{duration}}. The interview will be conducted in {{language}} and will be audio-recorded. [for questionnaires] You will be invited to complete a questionnaire about {{questionnaire_topics}}, which will take approximately {{duration}}. The data collection process will start in {{start}} and last until {{end}}.

Your participation in this project is entirely voluntary and you can withdraw at any time during the project without any negative consequences. The data collected will be treated with utmost confidentiality and anonymity. You will not be identified in the project report. [for video-recordings] Video recording is a group recording. If any students do not agree to be video-recorded, they will sit outside the camera view or have their face blurred. [for recordings] You [and your students] will have the right to review the recordings and erase part of or the entire recording if you wish. All the data collected will be securely stored in {{storage}}. Data containing personal identifiers will be destroyed {{retention_identifiable}} after the publication of the first paper from the project.

Please complete the reply slip below to indicate whether you would participate in the study. If you have any questions about the research, please do not hesitate to contact me (phone: {{researcher_phone}}; email: {{researcher_email}}) or my research project supervisor, {{supervisor_name}} ({{supervisor_email}}). If you would like to know more about the rights as a research participant, please contact the Human Research Ethics Committee, the University of Hong Kong (3917-5267).

Your help is very much appreciated.

Yours sincerely,

{{researcher_name}}
Faculty of Education
The University of Hong Kong

---

Reply Slip

Name of Participant: ______________________

◻ I agree to participate in this project and agree to the recording during the procedure.

◻ I agree to participate in this project, but do not agree to the recording during the procedure.

◻ I do not agree to participate in this project.

Signature: ______________________　　Date: ______________

HREC Reference Number: [The reference number is indicated in the letter of approval for ethical clearance issued by the Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC).]
```

If no recording: replace the three boxes with `I _________________________________ (Name of Participant) understand the procedures described above and agree to participate in this project.`

For a professor / expert interviewee, change the salutation to `Dear Professor {{surname}},` and keep everything else.

## §C　Principal, letter format

```
[School name & address]

Dear Principal,

I am {{pi_title}} {{pi_name}}, {{pi_position}} in the Faculty of Education at the University of Hong Kong. I will conduct a research project titled "{{title_en}}" and would like to invite your school to participate. The purpose of the study is to {{purpose_lay}}. The project will help {{benefits_wider_short}}.

{{grade}} students who participate in this research will be invited to {{procedures_students_summary}}. [Teachers who participate will {{procedures_teachers_summary}}.] [These activities will take place {{during lessons / in students' spare time}} and will not affect students' normal classes.] [The interviews will be audio-recorded; participants have the right to review the recordings and erase part of or the entire recording.] The data collection process will start in {{start}} and last until {{end}}.

All the information that is obtained during this research project will be kept strictly confidential, and students' privacy will be protected to the maximum extent allowable by law. The school and individual participants will not be identified in any report or publication. Your help is very much appreciated.

Yours sincerely,

{{pi_name}}
Faculty of Education
The University of Hong Kong
```

## §D　Parent / guardian

```
Parent/Guardian Consent Form

[Date]

Dear Parent/Guardian,

I am {{pi_title}} {{pi_name}}, {{pi_position}} in the Faculty of Education at the University of Hong Kong. I will conduct a research project entitled "{{title_en}}" and would like to invite your child to participate. [Your child's school has agreed to take part in the study.]

PURPOSE OF THE STUDY

The purpose of this study is to {{purpose_lay}}.

PROCEDURES

Your child will be asked to {{procedures_child}}. [These activities will take place during regular lessons and will not add to your child's workload.] [The interview will be audio-recorded; no video will be recorded.] The data collection process will start in {{start}} and last until {{end}}.

POTENTIAL RISKS / DISCOMFORTS AND THEIR MINIMIZATION

This study involves no known physical, psychological or social risks; all activities are comparable to regular classroom learning tasks. Your child is free to refuse to answer any question that s/he does not want to answer and to withdraw at any time without any consequences. This research project has nothing to do with his/her school study. Refusing to participate in this study will not have any impact on his/her grades in {{subject}}. [Please note that the researcher is also your child's {{subject}} teacher. Declining or withdrawing from the study will not affect your child's grades, school assessments, or relationship with the teacher. Non-participating students will still do the same classroom tasks, but no data from them will be collected.]

POTENTIAL BENEFITS

{{benefits_child}}

CONFIDENTIALITY

All the information that is obtained during this research project will be kept strictly confidential, and your child's privacy will be protected to the maximum extent allowable by law. To protect your child's privacy, his/her {{data_types}} will be stored on {{storage}}. Pseudonyms will be used when storing the data electronically and in any report or publication. The information obtained in this study will be used for research purposes only, and your child's responses will be accessed by and only by the research team. Data containing personal identifiers will be destroyed {{retention_identifiable}} after the publication of the first paper from the project. A copy of the research results after this project is completed will be given to you upon your request.

PARTICIPATION AND WITHDRAWAL

Your child's participation is totally voluntary. This means that your child can choose to stop at any time without negative consequences. Upon your request, your child's responses will be made available to you, and you have the right to review and erase all or part of the data [including the audio recordings]. In addition to your consent, your child will be given a simple, age-appropriate explanation and asked whether he or she is willing to take part.

QUESTIONS AND CONCERNS

If you have any questions or concerns about the research, please feel free to contact {{pi_title}} {{pi_name}} at {{pi_phone}} or {{pi_email}}. If you have questions about your rights as the guardian of a research participant and the rights of your child as a research participant, contact the Human Research Ethics Committee, HKU (3917-5267).

Your help is very much appreciated.

Yours sincerely,

{{pi_title}} {{pi_name}}
Faculty of Education
The University of Hong Kong
```

## §F　Oral / email consent script（when written consent is impractical; HREC Q11）

```
Consent Script

Thank you for agreeing to talk with me today. Before we begin, I would like to confirm a few things. This interview is part of a research study titled "{{title_en}}" conducted by {{pi_title}} {{pi_name}} at the University of Hong Kong. It will take about {{duration}} and will be audio-recorded. Your participation is voluntary; you can skip any question or stop at any time without any negative consequences. Your name and any identifying details will be replaced with a pseudonym in all reports. Recordings and transcripts will be kept on {{storage}} and data containing personal identifiers will be destroyed {{retention_identifiable}} after the first publication.

Do you agree to take part in this interview? [wait for "Yes"]
Do you agree to be audio-recorded? [wait for "Yes"]

Thank you. I am now starting the recording. Could you please state again that you agree to participate and to be recorded?
```

Email variant: paste the §A text into the email body and end with `Please reply "I agree to participate and to be audio-recorded" to confirm your consent.` Keep the reply in the project records as the consent record.

## Reusable sentences（drop-in）

- Voluntary: `Your participation is voluntary. This means that you can choose to stop at any time without negative consequences.`
- Recording rights: `You have the right to review the audio recording(s) and request the deletion of part or all of the recording(s).`
- Group video: `Video recording is a group recording. If any students do not agree to be video-recorded, they will sit outside the camera view or have their face blurred.`
- Confidentiality: `All the information that is obtained during this research project will be kept strictly confidential, and your privacy will be protected to the maximum extent allowable by law.`
- Legal limit (optional, stronger): `While all efforts will be made to maintain confidentiality, absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed; under applicable laws, the researcher may be required to disclose research data to relevant legal authorities.`
- Research use only: `The information obtained in the study will be used for research purposes only.`
- Pseudonyms: `Pseudonyms will be used in all research reports, theses, conference presentations, and publications.`
- Results copy: `A copy of the research results after this project is completed will be given to you upon your request.`
- No performance assessment: `The study will not assess your teaching performance; it focuses only on your experiences.`
- Not extra workload: `None of the tasks are extra workload; they are part of the regular curriculum.`
- HREC: `If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the Human Research Ethics Committee, HKU (3917-5267).`
